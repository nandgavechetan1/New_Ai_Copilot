💡 The Problem

What Students Struggle With

Most students enter the job market without a clear roadmap.

  • ❓ Don’t know which career path is right for their skill set
  • 📄 Upload a resume but don’t know if it’s missing critical keywords or skills
  • 🎯 No way to compare their skills against actual job requirements
  • 📚 Don’t know what to learn next or in what order
  • 💼 Never practiced a real technical or HR interview before applying
  • 📊 No objective measure of how job-ready they actually are
The result: Graduates apply to mismatched roles, fail interviews on avoidable gaps, and lose months of progress that a clear plan could have saved.

🛡 AI Hallucination Guard — Key Innovation

A deterministic post-processing filter strips any skill from “Strong” that the student didn’t explicitly have in their resume. Hallucinated skills are automatically relocated to “Missing”. The AI can never show fake strengths.

Safety Layers

Dual-mode: GPT + Fallback

Every AI call has a rule-based fallback. App works 100% without an API key — perfect for hackathon demos.

Hallucination Guard

Post-processing filter validates all AI output against the student’s actual skill list before display.

safeParseJSON

Repairs truncated AI JSON responses automatically — prevents 500 errors on long AI outputs.

⭐ Impact & Innovation

Why This Matters

Six key innovations that differentiate AI Career Copilot.

🎯

Personalized, Not Generic

Every analysis, roadmap, and recommendation is computed from the student’s actual resume, skills, and GitHub — not a template.

🛡

Hallucination-Free Results

Deterministic post-processing ensures the AI can never show skills the student doesn’t have as strengths.

🔄

Complete Career Loop

Resume → Skills → Career → Skill Gap → Roadmap → Interview → Dashboard. One continuous journey.

🚀

Works Without AI API Key

Full rule-based fallback means the app is 100% demonstrable at any hackathon, even without OpenAI access.

📱

Production UI Quality

Responsive TailwindCSS design — works on mobile, tablet, desktop. Professional SaaS-level interface.

🔒

Honest AI — No False Promises

Scores are labelled as AI guidance. The system never fabricates experience or guarantees employment.
